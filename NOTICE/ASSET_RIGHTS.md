# Asset Rights Notice

This package is designed for open-source publication without redistributing third-party screenshots or copyrighted character assets.

## Included Assets

The images under `assets/` are generated/original experiment outputs from the local cover committee workflow:

- ordinary comparison boards;
- debug/map boards;
- final v73 cover exports;
- edge evidence images.

They are included so readers can inspect the iteration process and reproduce the method.

## Excluded Assets

Do not add these to the open repository:

- third-party Xiaohongshu screenshots;
- creator profile screenshots;
- Disney/Zootopia or other copyrighted character images;
- assets that imply endorsement by a platform, creator, or rights holder.

## Reference Learning Boundary

Reference accounts may be summarized as design observations: title structure, contrast, hierarchy, density, and layout grammar. Do not copy their screenshots, captions, likeness, private data, or distinctive protected artwork into the repo.

## Publishing Checklist

Before pushing to GitHub:

- run `node scripts/check-package.mjs`;
- inspect new images for third-party screenshots or copyrighted materials;
- confirm repository license;
- confirm whether assets share the same license as docs/scripts or need a separate license.
